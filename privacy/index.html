Privacy notice

This notice covers Global Goods DC Inc. (Canada) and USA Goods DC Inc. (New Jersey, United States), two affiliated, separately incorporated companies. Last updated November 2026.

This website

  • The site has no cookies, analytics, tracking or forms.
  • Its typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your browser's request, including your IP address.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.
  • If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to handle the business you raise with us.

Suppliers and business partners

We keep the business contact details, terms, price lists and documents our suppliers share with us for purchasing, accounting and our legal obligations. We treat supplier names, pricing and terms as confidential and don't publish them.

Amazon information

Each company uses Amazon's Selling Partner API only to operate its own seller account: inventory, orders, listings, fees, fulfillment and purchasing plans.

  • We don't sell Amazon information, and we don't disclose it to third parties other than service providers that host our own systems.
  • Amazon fulfills our customer orders. Where Amazon reports include customer details, we don't keep them: we use only the product, date, quantity and status of each order.
  • Access is limited to each company's owner. Access credentials are kept out of our code, and information travels over encrypted connections.
  • We keep Amazon information only as long as our operations, accounting and legal obligations require.
  • If a security incident affects Amazon information, we notify Amazon as its policies require.
