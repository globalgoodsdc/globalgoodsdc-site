What we do for the brands we carry

We are a wholesale customer and an Amazon.ca retailer. We buy your products, keep them in stock and sell them well.

Wholesale purchasing

Branded consumer products bought outright with our own capital, in wholesale quantities and on agreed terms.

Inventory and replenishment

Demand monitoring and forecasting guide each reorder, to help products stay available through Canada's seasonal peaks.

Amazon FBA operations

Every unit prepared and labelled to Amazon's requirements and shipped into its Canadian fulfillment network.

Catalog and ASIN management

Each product matched to the correct ASIN and pack size. Catalog errors we find are reported to Amazon and to you.

Brand and listing compliance support

Help identifying and reporting listing discrepancies through Amazon's processes, and respect for the brand and MAP terms we agree to.

Data-driven purchasing

Our own software estimates demand and profitability after Amazon fees before we buy, and plans replenishment after.

How a partnership starts

  1. Introduce your line

    Send your catalog or price list with UPCs, case packs, minimums and any reseller or MAP policies.

  2. Open an account

    We complete your account application for Global Goods DC Inc. and agree on terms and lead times.

  3. First purchase order

    We order, receive and prepare the goods, and send them into Amazon's Canadian fulfillment network.

  4. Grow the line

    As products sell, we reorder, share what's working and look at more of your range together.

What you can expect from us

Clear purchase orders

Every order is written down: products, quantities, prices and the entity being invoiced.

Payment as agreed

We pay on the terms we accept. If anything needs to change, we raise it before the invoice is due.

Legitimate sourcing

We buy from manufacturers, brand owners and their distributors, and we keep the paperwork.

Respect for your terms

Reseller agreements, territories and MAP policies we agree to are policies we follow.

Complete account applications

We supply the corporate and tax documentation your credit or dealer application requires, and references where our suppliers agree.

Prompt, honest communication

Questions get a direct answer, and we tell you plainly how your products are performing on Amazon.ca.
